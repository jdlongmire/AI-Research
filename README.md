## Special Focus

**AI Dunning-Kruger (AIDK) Framework**

A theoretical framework for understanding the structural epistemic limitations of Large Language Models. AIDK describes the permanent architectural condition where AI systems produce uniform confidence regardless of reliability, lack mechanisms for detecting competence boundaries, and cannot self-correct through encounter with reality.

Key concepts:
- **AIDK** — AI Dunning-Kruger: structural epistemic limitation (architectural, not correctable)
- **IDKE** — Interactive Dunning-Kruger Effect: amplification when AI limitations meet human limitations
- **HCAE** — Human-Curated, AI-Enabled: deployment framework stratified by epistemic authority
- **MAPT** — Model Advanced Persistent Threat: security framing for AIDK

**Publication:** [AI Dunning-Kruger (AIDK): A Framework for Understanding Structural Epistemic Limitations in AI Systems](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18316059) (Zenodo, January 2026)
